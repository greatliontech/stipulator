# The solo rule lives in four places

A witness's observation proof attaches only to a process that ran the
subject alone. Three sites predict that at discovery — captureGroup.solo
(one runnable, one name), the selective form's candidate gate (one stale
name in the package), the drift retry's gate (one retried subject in the
package) — and the per-subject judgment now observes it from the
granting process's rows. The predictions decide who becomes a proof
candidate; the observation decides whether the proof attaches; today
they agree by construction, and a change to either side can silently
disagree. One rule — the rows-derived fact deciding candidacy after
execution, or the prediction as the one source the judgment reads —
deletes three.
