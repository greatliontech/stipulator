# Conditional performance evidence and acceptance

Lands: tooling-redesign 9.4

Activation condition: a corpus declares a concrete performance requirement
with a deciding acceptance workflow. The census preserves this condition;
it does not commission an evidence tier merely for tool symmetry.

The current clause kinds and evidence ladder in `docs/specs/evidence.md`
and the `internal/coverage` policy judgment have no performance-measurement
class. The original proposal considered Pew recordings as an input to such
a class. Their applicability can establish that recorded measurements are
usable under their declared conditions; it does not establish an acceptable
latency, regression threshold, confidence rule or harmless empirical noise.

At activation, specify the measurement obligation, binding identity,
compatibility, uncertainty, missing-history behavior and explicit threshold
authority. Preserve original producing evidence and consumer-owned statistical
acceptance rather than mapping every fresh recording directly to covered.
REQ-core-claims-untrusted applies to imported claims. Secondary constituents:
8.1/8.2 govern historical authority and variation; 7.6 separates information
from enforcing performance gates. Stale or unsupported evidence grants no
acceptance, and fresh evidence alone supplies no threshold.
