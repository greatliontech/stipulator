# A gap cannot say "the tree is witnessed to contradict this requirement"

Lands: user decision (bldc consumer report 2026-09-05).

A gap record means "unwitnessed": the requirement has no witness, or
its witness class is not what the cell admits, and the reason says why
until the landing condition fires. A consumer has a second case the
same record has to carry: a requirement promoted to spec tier as the
contract, whose letter the shipped tree contradicts by design until a
named piece of work lands. Two instances in bldc on 2026-09-05:

- `REQ-model-space-enclosure` — the contract says a space's outline is
  derived from its enclosure and never authored; the shipped schema
  carries an authored outline, and every rule reads it. Gap declared
  with `Lands: the space-enclosure train lands the derivation`.
- `REQ-core-engine-schema-generic` — bound to a kind-literal layering
  test that guards every engine package; one engine package is
  excused as deferred because it names two kinds until the schema can
  declare the affordances. Gap declared on the structural cell with
  the excuse stated in the reason.

Both read green in `check`: the gap excuses `uncovered`, and nothing
but the reason's prose says "witnessed to fail, by design, until
<condition>" rather than "no witness yet". The distinction matters to
the reader of the summary — a contradicted requirement is a known
debt with a trigger, an unwitnessed one is a coverage hole — and to
the landing: a contradicted gap should not resolve on a witness that
passes while the condition is unfired, because a passing witness
against a contradicted letter is exactly the vacuous test the record
exists to catch.

Ask: a declared state beside the gap — `contradicted`, carrying the
landing condition like a gap — reported distinctly in the summary
(its own count and rows), and refusing resolution until the condition
is fired explicitly, at which point the requirement wants a witness
like any other. The reason field keeps saying why.
