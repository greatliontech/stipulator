# A record whose manifest does not decode is dropped at load without a reason

A witness record whose runtime-input manifest fails the load's shape
judgment (it does not decode as canonical Gofresh v1) is that record
alone absent: the subject executes and no re-execution reason names why,
on either evidence form. Handing such a record to the serving rounds
instead would let gofresh's fingerprint tier attribute it — the engine
turns an unrecomputable manifest into its own `runtimeinputs` stale
reason without faulting the group — at the cost of the record's `passed`
key entering the flip index (REQ-evidence-flip-environment) and one
derivation per group where the load derives once. The choice is between
a silent drop with one derivation and an attributed refusal with
gofresh's vocabulary; the serving ladder's re-shaping is where it lands.
