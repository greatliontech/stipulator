# Long-Pole Width Starvation (`GOMAXPROCS=2`), Width/Flavor-Sensitive Test Failures in `internal/backends/golang` & `deniedTests` 553-Solo Rerun Amplification

- **Status:** Open
- **Governing contract:** [`docs/specs/evidence.md`](../specs/evidence.md) (`REQ-evidence-witness-freshness-concurrency`, `REQ-evidence-witness-freshness-isolation`, `REQ-evidence-witness-freshness-package-process`, `REQ-evidence-outcome-premises`), [`docs/specs/backends/go.md`](../specs/backends/go.md) (`REQ-go-retained-test-execution`)
- **Lands:** Chunk 3 in [`docs/plans/plan.md`](../plans/plan.md)

## Problem

1. **Bundling 36 Packages into One `race` Invocation Throttles `internal/backends/golang` to `GOMAXPROCS=2` (`48m12s` Long Pole)**
   - Under `REQ-evidence-witness-freshness-concurrency` ([`internal/backends/golang/admission.go`](../../internal/backends/golang/admission.go) lines 489–520, [`observe.go`](../../internal/backends/golang/observe.go) lines 173–181), `SpawnBound = min(processors/2, packages) = min(16, 36) = 16` on a 32-thread host, fixing `GOMAXPROCS = 32 / 16 = 2` in `WitnessEnv`.
   - In `.stipulator/policy.textproto`, `35` of the `36` packages in `race` complete within `5m45s`, leaving `internal/backends/golang` (`719` sequential integration tests spawning child `go test -c -race`, `go list`, and `gofresh` SSA/RTA analyses that inherit `GOMAXPROCS=2`) running alone for **`48m12s`** while 30 of 32 host cores sit idle.

2. **Six Tests in `internal/backends/golang` Deadlock, Fail, or Skip Under `GOMAXPROCS=2` and Custom `godst` Toolchains**
   - `TestServedRecordRevalidatesAfterTheRunsExecutions` ([`internal/backends/golang/freshness_test.go`](../../internal/backends/golang/freshness_test.go) lines 900–1010): spawns an inner 2-package `RunWitnesses` (`reader` and `writer`) where `writer` polls for 60s waiting for `reader` to persist concurrently (`SpawnBound >= 2`). Inheriting `GOMAXPROCS=2` causes the inner run to compute `witnessSpawnBound(2, 2) = min(max(1, 2/2), 2) = 1`, serializing `writer` and `reader` and deadlocking for 60s before failing with `the reader's package never persisted`.
   - `TestGoScopedAllocationWarmWidthMovement` and `TestGoScopedAllocationWarmUnobservedWidthMovement` ([`internal/backends/golang/allocation_integration_test.go`](../../internal/backends/golang/allocation_integration_test.go) lines 146–170): call `if runtime.GOMAXPROCS(0) < 4 { t.Skip(...) }`, skipping both witness-bound tests whenever parent width is `< 4`.
   - `TestSelectionNoticesAttributeUnwalkedSelections` ([`internal/backends/golang/selectionnotices_test.go`](../../internal/backends/golang/selectionnotices_test.go) lines 34–54): skips when the ambient toolchain (`godst`) hits `unauditedTagGOROOT`, leaving `REQ-check-policy-notices` unwitnessed under `godst`.
   - `TestAdmissionsOfOneProcessShareTheHostGate` ([`internal/backends/golang/admission_test.go`](../../internal/backends/golang/admission_test.go) lines 1126–1420): relies on `time.Sleep(50ms)` / `time.Sleep(100ms)` after `admitAsync` (`lines 1190, 1404, 1413`) to assume goroutines have parked inside `g.cond.Wait()`, flaking under `GOMAXPROCS=2` + `-race`.
   - `TestGoExecuteEnvelopeTimeoutNamesAbortedTests` ([`internal/backends/golang/execute_unix_test.go`](../../internal/backends/golang/execute_unix_test.go) lines 26–62): sets `n.Timeout = 2 * time.Second` (`line 37`) across the entire `ExecuteInvocation` (including `CaptureProducerFrame`, `engine.Snapshot`, `PrepareTestUnit`, and `-race` binary startup), expiring before `=== RUN TestHang` emits under `GOMAXPROCS=2`.

3. **`deniedTests` Schedules `553` Sequential Solo Reruns and Retains Red-Batch `2.1 GiB` `testlog.txt` Until `afterSlot` (`selection.go:247-302, 358-400`)**
   - When `3` of `559` batch tests fail (`HEALTH_DISPOSITION_TEST_FAILED`), `deniedTests` ([`internal/backends/golang/selection.go`](../../internal/backends/golang/selection.go) lines 358–400) marks all `553` completed passing tests as denied and re-executes them sequentially one by one in `553` separate `package.test` processes (`~50` additional minutes).
   - Meanwhile, `inSlot` (`lines 247–282`) retains the failed batch's `2.1 GiB` `testlog.txt` and all `553` solo `testlog.txt` files on `/tmp` until `afterSlot` (`lines 287–302`), which then runs `finishNativeRun` (`Unit.Close` -> full `2.1 GiB` `testlog` parse + `Validate`) on the red batch attempt even though a red process yields zero green witness evidence (`REQ-evidence-witness-freshness-isolation`).

## Soundness Constraints & Resolution Scope

1. **Core/Leaf Policy Split**: Split `.stipulator/policy.textproto` into `race:core` (`./internal/backends/golang`, `./internal/cmd`, giving `SpawnBound = 2` -> `GOMAXPROCS = 16` on 32 cores) and `race:leaf` (remaining fast packages), mirroring `gofresh/.stipulator/policy.textproto`.
2. **Width & Flavor Test Determinism**: Make all 6 tests in `internal/backends/golang` deterministic across processor widths and toolchain flavors (`godst` and vanilla Go alike): explicitly supply inner `GOMAXPROCS >= 4` for multi-slot concurrency tests, replace `time.Sleep` with deterministic gate-parking hooks in `admission_test.go`, budget preparation separately from hang execution in `execute_unix_test.go`, and decouple `selectionnotices_test.go` from `unauditedTagGOROOT` skips.
3. **Unified `inSlot`/`afterSlot` Attempt Lifecycle, Red-Batch Accounting & Isolation Cleanup (`REQ-evidence-outcome-premises`, `ExecuteSelection`)**:
   - Consolidate the duplicated `inSlot` and `afterSlot` native execution, solo-isolation, and `testlog` finalization closures across `executeInvocationPrepared` ([`internal/backends/golang/execute.go`](../../internal/backends/golang/execute.go) lines 319–393) and `executeSelectionObserved` ([`internal/backends/golang/selection.go`](../../internal/backends/golang/selection.go) lines 215–302) onto one shared unit/attempt lifecycle helper.
   - Per `REQ-evidence-outcome-premises` ([`docs/specs/evidence.md`](../specs/evidence.md) lines 690–694) and [`internal/backends/golang/observe.go`](../../internal/backends/golang/observe.go) lines 131–156, a normally completed failing test process still establishes process completion (`ProcessObservation.Completed`) while granting zero passing witness records; skip `testlog.txt` parsing/validation on red attempts without falsely marking a normally completed process as aborted/incomplete, and unlink `logPath` immediately.
   - Finalize and unlink each isolation attempt's `testlog` inside `inSlot` rather than retaining hundreds of logs until `afterSlot`, and evaluate whether `ExecuteSelection` (`selection.go` lines 24–28, 68–70) should try a reduced batch of completed-passing tests (excluding failed/aborted tests) before per-test solo fallback while preserving `ProcessOutcome` contracts.
